How Picky decides

  1. Each rule is sorted once into Hide or Only show. Click the chip in the popup to flip it.
  2. Every item in your feed is one Jev request, with all your rules asked together. If Jev isn’t sure and the item has a picture, it asks once more with the picture.
  3. A Hide match at 0.65 or above hides the item. With any Only show rule, items that match none of them are hidden.
  4. Anything between 0.35 and 0.65 is blurred instead, so you decide.
